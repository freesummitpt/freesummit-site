The activity

A half day of sport climbing at Forte do Cavalo, also called Sesimbra Velha, next to the old fort and the Cavalo lighthouse just west of Sesimbra town.

The crag has about 75 bolted routes right above the sea. We base the day in Portugal dos Pequeninos, the most popular sector, with routes of around 14 m. You climb on top-rope, with the guide setting every anchor and checking every belay.

Good for beginners with some sporting background and for people who already climb. Pair it with the Pinheirinhos via ferrata, a few minutes away, for a full day in Sesimbra.

The plan

  1. MeetWelcome, gear fitting and safety briefing by the fort.
  2. To the cragAbout 10 minutes on foot to the Portugal dos Pequeninos sector.
  3. SkillsKnots, belaying and climbing calls. The guide checks every belay.
  4. ClimbRoutes of rising difficulty above the sea, taking turns climbing and belaying.
  5. FinishPack up, walk back and debrief.

What to bring

  • Shoes: trainers for the walk in (climbing shoes are provided).
  • Clothes: comfortable sports clothing that lets you move freely.
  • Water: 1 to 1.5 L and a snack.
  • Sun: sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap.
  • Backpack: a small one.
