The activity

The step from trying climbing to being a climber. Over four days on the crags around Lisbon you learn everything you need to climb outdoors with a partner, without a guide.

We start with movement, knots and belaying on top-rope, then move on to lead climbing: clipping quickdraws, falling safely, lowering off and cleaning the anchor at the top. Each day builds on the one before, with lots of time on the rock.

Each day takes place at a different crag in Arrábida, Sesimbra, Cascais or Sintra, chosen for the weather and the group, so you also get to know the best climbing near Lisbon.

The plan

  1. Day 1 · BasicsGear, knots, belaying and climbing movement on top-rope.
  2. Day 2 · TechniqueFootwork, reading routes and first steps leading.
  3. Day 3 · LeadingClipping, falling practice and belaying a leader.
  4. Day 4 · On your ownCleaning anchors, lowering off and climbing in pairs, with the guide watching.

What to bring

  • Shoes: trainers for the walk in (climbing shoes are provided).
  • Clothes: comfortable sports clothing and a warm layer.
  • Food: lunch, snacks and 1.5 L of water each day.
  • Sun: sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap.
