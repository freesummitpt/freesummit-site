How the day goes

About 4 hours from meeting to finish. We handle the safety and the gear; you just bring the basics.

The plan

  1. MeetWelcome, gear fitting and safety briefing.
  2. ApproachShort walk to the crag or the start of the via ferrata.
  3. SkillsKnots, belaying and climbing calls. The guide checks every belay.
  4. ClimbRoutes of rising difficulty, taking turns, with photo stops.
  5. FinishWalk back, gear return and debrief. We send you the photos afterwards.

What to bring

  • Shoes: trainers or trail shoes with good grip. No sandals.
  • Clothes: comfortable sports clothing; a light jacket in cooler months.
  • Water: 1 to 1.5 L and a snack.
  • Sun: sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap.
  • Backpack: a small one is enough.
  • We bring: harness, helmet, climbing shoes, via ferrata lanyards, ropes and first aid.
