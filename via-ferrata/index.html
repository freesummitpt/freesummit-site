The activity

A half-day via ferrata along the limestone cliffs west of Sesimbra, inside the Arrábida Natural Park, with the Luiz Saldanha Marine Park below you the whole way.

The route traverses the cliffs on steel cables, rungs and some fixed ropes. You are clipped in at all times with an energy-absorbing lanyard, and your guide is beside you on every section, with a rope for the harder steps.

It is very exposed and very beautiful. It suits active adults and teenagers who feel fine with heights and can walk and scramble for 3 to 4 hours.

Included

  • Guide on every section
  • Harness, helmet and via ferrata lanyard set (EN 958)
  • Via ferrata gloves
  • Rope and kit for short belays on hard steps
  • Activity insurance

The plan

  1. MeetWelcome at the Pinheirinhos parking, safety briefing and gear fitting.
  2. ApproachAbout 45 minutes on coastal paths down to the start.
  3. Via ferrataThe cliff traverse, with photo stops over the sea.
  4. Walk backAbout 40 minutes back to the parking.
  5. FinishGear return and debrief.

What to bring

  • Shoes: trail or approach shoes with good grip. No sandals.
  • Clothes: sports clothing; a light jacket in cooler months.
  • Water: 1 to 1.5 L and a snack.
  • Sun: sunscreen, sunglasses and a cap.
  • Backpack: a small one.
